Pin the extension

Click the puzzle piece 🧩 next to your address bar, then the pin beside Catch Up for Twitch. The icon shows your unread count and opens the inbox. The popup also holds the quick settings: how many days to look back, and your sleep hours.

2

Connect your Twitch account

A one-time standard Twitch login, needed because Twitch's API requires it to read your follow list and each channel's past broadcasts. Your login stays in your browser. No tracking, no data collection, nothing sent anywhere else.

⚠ The inbox stays empty until this is done. One click, ten seconds.

Connected! You're all set.

Pick your channels
3

Pick channels and catch up

Tick the channels you care about. From then on the inbox lists every VOD from the last 7 days, marks what you've watched, and remembers where you stopped so "Catch up" resumes from that spot.

Your access token is stored only in this browser and can be revoked any time from your Twitch account's Connections settings.

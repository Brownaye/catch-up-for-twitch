# Privacy Policy — Catch Up for Twitch

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Catch Up for Twitch is a browser extension that lists recent past
broadcasts (VODs) from Twitch channels you choose, and remembers which
ones you have watched.

## What we collect

When you connect your Twitch account, Twitch provides the extension
with an access token. This token is stored only in your browser's
local extension storage. The channels you tick, your settings (lookback
window and sleep hours), which VODs you have marked as watched, and the
playback position of VODs you have started are also stored locally, and
nowhere else.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not operate any servers. No data is transmitted to us.
- We do not collect, sell, or share any personal information.
- We do not track your browsing. The only pages the extension reads
  are Twitch VOD pages (twitch.tv/videos/…), and only to save how far
  you have watched.

## Who the extension talks to

The extension communicates only with Twitch's official services
(id.twitch.tv for login, api.twitch.tv for your follow list, channel
VODs and live status), and only to build your inbox.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes all locally stored data. You can
also revoke the extension's access at any time from your Twitch
account settings under Connections.
